## TL;DR

1. Doing harder things
2. Practicing mindfulness
3. Fixing sleep schedule
4. Playing cognitive games, working out, and playing sports

The greatest sin of our times is that we have fcked up Attention Span for nothing.

Most of us, or at least I have a hard time navigating through daily tasks without getting distracted and losing focus. The main contributing factors of this fcked up attention span are consuming short-form content, coupled with many unhealthy lifestyle habits such as having a poor sleep cycle. The fact that I am writing this at midnight is ironically funny.

I think understanding the brain pathways that are used when we **focus** might help us fix them.

### Dopamine — The Hype Guy

Dopamine is the hype guy. Imagine a guy who hypes you to do something that you might not have done otherwise.

Dopamine is more process-oriented, and not result-oriented. Dopamine makes you believe, *"You will feel good if you scroll reels."*

We get easier wins, and our hype guy hypes us to win easily and get that relief or happiness.

### Fix 1: Do Harder Things

Do things that require sustained effort:

- Run a 5K
- Read a book without taking your phone
- Do math
- Learn something difficult

The idea is to deliberately practice staying with tasks that don't provide instant gratification.

---

### The Brain Is Not a Computer

The brain is more like a computer — the more load you give it, the slower it becomes.

Thinking is good, but overthinking is bad. Loading the brain with lots of information can make it feel slower, since it is not designed by nature to handle such complex bombardment of thoughts continuously.

### Fix 2: Practice Mindfulness

Take life easier.

**Think less. Think slow. Think simple.**

Mindfulness can help you become more aware of where your attention is going instead of constantly reacting to every thought, notification, or distraction.

---

### Fix 3: Sleep, Sleep, Sleep

When we have dark circles under our eyes due to lack of sleep, the brain suffers too.

The **Default Mode Network (DMN)** is associated with internally focused thought, such as mind-wandering, remembering, and daydreaming. When you're tired, staying focused on simple day-to-day tasks becomes harder, and you may experience brief lapses in attention or even microsleeps.

**Sleep 8 hours. Sleep at night. Sleep properly.**

---

### Fix 4: Train Your Brain and Body

The brain slows down due to various other reasons linked to stress, lifestyle, environment, and circumstances.

Sometimes the best way to fix it is by taking everything slow while still moving everything forward.

Fixing attention span and focus requires working on many cognitive abilities, including:

- Working memory
- Selective attention
- Processing speed
- Fluid intelligence
- Cognitive flexibility
- Visuospatial skills

Play cognitive training games and sports in general.

Play:

- Chess
- Badminton
- Cricket
- Sudoku
- Other games that require strategy, coordination, memory, or sustained attention

And, of course, **work out**.

---

## Attention Is the Currency

The most valuable currency of today's world is **Attention**.

Just like saving money, we should be saving ourselves some attention.

Our attention is constantly being pulled in a thousand different directions. Maybe the goal isn't to become productive every second of the day.

Maybe the goal is simply to have the ability to **choose where our attention goes.**
